What's actually driving this page

EngageThe stage switcher stands in for FloStack's rule engine — in production it reads explicit signals (form answers, application status from the CRM) and re-renders headline, CTA, and urgency copy per stage. Here, the chatbot and voicebot can also trigger it directly when they detect intent.
GatherThe activity chips, chatbot, and voicebot all write into the same context object in real time — topics discussed, pages explored, stage signals. Nothing here is a dropdown standing in for behavior; clicking and chatting are the behavior.
CentraliseThe checklist and context strip aren't just UI — they're the same record a rep would see in the CRM, so a human conversation starts from where the microsite and bots left off instead of from zero.
InsightNot shown here: a funnel view of stage-to-stage conversion across every applicant, benchmarked against industry melt rates — that's the ROI case for the customer.
