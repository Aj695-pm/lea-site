The Product

Built to close the gap between a booked visit and a completed one.

HealthFlo layers on top of the systems you already run — it doesn't ask you to rip and replace. Four connected capabilities cover the journey from first click to a visit that actually happens.

📅

Book on Your Terms

Patients pick a provider and a time that works for them — any hour, from their phone — instead of calling during business hours and waiting to find out who's even available.

The booking happens on the patient's schedule, not the front desk's.

Matched With the Right Provider

How It Works

From inquiry to a completed visit — without a second phone call.

HealthFlo sits between your marketing forms and your front desk, turning an inquiry into a visit that actually happens, automatically.

1

Patient inquires

A prospective patient fills out any form — on your site, an ad landing page, or a referral intake link.

2

A matched booking page generates instantly

HealthFlo builds a personalized page in under a second — the right provider, open slots, and any coverage or cost questions answered inline.

3

Patient books and clears the visit

They pick a slot, get their questions answered, and settle whatever the visit requires — a deposit, a copay, an eligibility check — in the same flow.

4

Care team follows up confirmed

Your front desk sees a visit that's already matched, scheduled, and cleared inside your CRM, and can spend the call on care — not logistics.
